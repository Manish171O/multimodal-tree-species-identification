# Results — Adaptive Quality-Aware Multimodal Tree Species Identification

> **All numbers are on SYNTHETIC demo data** (NEON-MMTC-like layout, generated by `src/data/synthetic.py`). They show that the pipeline works and how the components interact. They are **not** estimates of real-world accuracy.

Site-held-out evaluation. Test sites: MLBS, STEI, WREF (n = 423). Final model chosen on **validation** macro-F1: **`spectral_aug`**. Single seed (42).

## 3b. Silent sensor corruption at test time — test accuracy (macro-F1)

A sensor is still *present*, but its input is corrupted. These corruptions were never seen in training: Gaussian noise of 2σ on RGB or HSI; LiDAR xyz jitter of 1σ with 80 % of points removed. This is where per-sample trust should matter most.
